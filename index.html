About the meeting

Groups in Action is a two-day gathering on the interplay between group theory and discrete structures: graphs, maps, polytopes, and other combinatorial objects with a high degree of symmetry. Talks run Thursday afternoon and all day Friday, with plenty of time for discussion in between.

Attendance is free, but please register so we can plan coffee breaks and room space.
